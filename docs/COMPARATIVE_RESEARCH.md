# Community-token mechanisms and V1PR

Comparisons describe mechanisms, not proof that a feature causes viral reach or price growth. V1PR's implemented release is defined in [the white paper](WHITEPAPER.md).

- [BONK's distribution paper](https://www.bonkcoin.com/BONK-Paper.pdf) illustrates a community-oriented allocation and explicitly earmarked liquidity. V1PR allocates 10% to approved free claims and 35% to initial/later liquidity reserves; an actual funded pool still requires paired assets.
- [Dogecoin's community guide](https://dogecoin.com/dogepedia/articles/how-can-i-help-doge/) emphasizes community participation and reusable creative work. V1PR's creator grants and Hunt Board use a distinct snake identity, with published Community awards and transaction records.
- [PulseX](https://pulsex.com/) describes buying and burning from exchange fees. V1PR does not operate a fee-producing exchange or tax unrelated swaps. Its implemented burns come from 20% of lock-exit fees, voluntary holder burns, and unclaimed inventory after expiry.
- [HEX's technical explainer](https://hex.com/_assets/docs/HEX_whitepaper.pdf) describes inflation-funded staking incentives. V1PR instead escrows pre-minted inventory for every accepted lock. Longer lock rates grow exponentially, but there is no new minting, compounding guarantee, or infinite reward stream.

First-come reward admission can encourage earlier participation because available capacity is finite. It cannot guarantee growth, a fair transaction queue, or an imminent exhaustion date. Each accepted lock receives a fixed funded token entitlement, while future locks can be rejected when the reserve is insufficient. Mature exits charge 0.3% of initial principal; the reward pool escrows a matching mature-fee offset, so each completed accepted term earns positive net V1PR before gas. Early exits can still return less than deposited.

Useful operating measures are claims paid, remaining reward capacity, outstanding escrow, actual burns, wallet concentration, exchange depth, LP control, and repeat creator participation. Follower counts and price alone do not establish a healthy community. No referral commissions or paid contribution campaign is included in the release.
